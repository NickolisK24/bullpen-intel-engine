## Part I - Executive Direction

### 1. North Star

Make BaseballOS the first place a baseball person checks to understand a bullpen before a game and the definitive page they keep open when they need one team or reliever in depth.

### 2. Protected Assets

- Canonical appearance and roster correctness.
- Three-state Team State vocabulary.
- Backend-owned public semantics.
- Fail-closed handling of genuinely unsupported claims.
- Historical immutability for published artifacts.
- Bullpen-only scope and non-predictive boundaries.
- Solo-founder maintainability.

### 3. De-emphasized Product Behaviors

- Repeated evidence blocks that duplicate the sentence above them.
- Trust/disclaimer language competing with the baseball answer.
- Roadmap work whose main outcome is documentation/ceremony rather than user or operational value.
- Surface-specific features that do not strengthen the main product spine.
- Infrastructure expansion before a demonstrated bottleneck.

## Part III - Immediate Execution Order

1. Close the scheduled trusted-publication proof already in flight; do not widen it.
2. Finalize Team State calibration so Fresh/Stretched/Vulnerable describe team-level operating reality rather than one-arm perfection/defect.
3. Freeze the new product hierarchy and visual system around Tonight -> League -> Team -> Pitcher -> History.
4. Implement Team Board 2.0 as the next major product initiative.
5. Build What Changed as part of Team Board, not as a later editorial add-on.
6. Build Tonight from the same team read models once Team Board semantics are stable.
7. Upgrade Pitcher pages and deployment/role modeling.
8. Add Matchup, search, history, then personalization.
9. Resume distribution infrastructure after the core product is worth distributing.
