## Part I - Editorial Identity

### 1. Voice

- Direct and baseball-literate.
- Specific rather than dramatic.
- Names teams and relievers.
- Uses real workload/usage context.
- Explains why the fact matters to the current bullpen picture.
- Avoids internal engine, trust, or software language unless the post is explicitly technical.

### 2. Default Content Formula

Baseball point -> named arms / receipts -> why the bullpen picture changed -> exact current destination. Freshness can be a compact date line. A limitation appears only when it materially changes interpretation.

## Part II - Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pregame Bullpen | Become useful before first pitch. | Two bullpens entering a matchup differently; rested late-inning group; heavy recent series burden. |
| Team Change | Create daily team habit. | What changed overnight; roster move; short start; recovery after off-day. |
| Reliever Usage | Own deployment and workload discussion. | Role movement; 3-in-4 pattern; multi-inning burden; leverage concentration. |
| Bullpen Structure | Explain how a bullpen is functioning. | Workload concentration, rotation transfer, active group churn, handedness/coverage. |
| Founder / Product | Build professional credibility without overwhelming baseball content. | Why a feature was removed, data-quality lesson, UX/product decision. |
| Evergreen Bullpen Education | Own search and category language. | How to read reliever workload, bullpen roles, short-start impact, recovery windows. |

## Part III - Platform Standards

### 3. X

Use timely, named, compact baseball observations. One team or game, one point, one or two receipts, one direct link. No generic platform promotion.

### 4. LinkedIn

Founder/product posts should still begin with the baseball or product problem. Technical depth follows the user consequence. The strongest posts show judgment, not feature counts.

### 5. Reddit

Community-specific baseball observation first. Product link only when useful and permitted. The post should remain worthwhile if the BaseballOS name is removed.

### 6. Instagram / Visual

Use clean, original bullpen visuals: team state, named arms, workload, role change, or matchup context. Avoid decorative baseball imagery, player likeness dependency, or poster-style hype.

### 7. Newsletter

When activated, the newsletter should read like a bullpen briefing: a few material developments, tonight context, and direct team links. It is not a changelog.

## Part IV - Share Artifacts

### 8. What a Shared Object Should Carry

- Clear team/game identity.
- The bullpen point in one sentence.
- Named arms or strongest facts.
- Data-through date.
- Exact Team Board, matchup, or historical share destination.

The visual does not need to reproduce a full evidence panel. It should preserve enough context that the claim remains understandable when separated from the website.

## Part V - Distribution Strategy

### 9. Distribution Follows Product Strength

Do not overinvest in renderer, metadata, cadence, or growth mechanics before the Team Board and Tonight experience are worth returning to. Distribution multiplies product quality; it cannot substitute for it.

### 10. Creator Workflow

1. Open Tonight or a Team Board.
2. Identify one genuinely non-obvious bullpen development.
3. Use the canonical named arms and current values.
4. Create the platform-native observation.
5. Link directly to the exact team/game/history destination.
6. Measure downstream team opens, returns, and citations rather than likes alone.

## Part VI - Editorial Boundaries

- No betting/picks/edge language.
- No fantasy recommendations.
- No private injury claims.
- No manager-intent claims.
- No future-reliever certainty.
- No quality ranking disguised as bullpen state.
- No internal score promotion.

## Part VII - Measurement

| Goal | Better signal |
| --- | --- |
| Pregame usefulness | Clicks before first pitch, matchup/team opens, returning users. |
| Team habit | Direct Team Board returns and bookmarks. |
| Creator value | Citations, screenshots with attribution, repeat use by the same creator. |
| Category ownership | Branded search, inbound “what does BaseballOS say?” behavior. |
| Founder credibility | Relevant sports-tech/data/product conversations. |
| Distribution quality | Downstream opens and evidence/history inspection, not raw impressions. |
